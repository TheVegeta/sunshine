- Typography: Editorial, high-fashion serif pairings for headings (evoking luxury lookbooks and handwritten poetry) contrasted with clean, refined humanist sans-serif for body copy and micro-interactions.

TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE (MULTI-PAGE HTML):
- Structure: Generate a true multi-page HTML architecture (NOT a Single Page Application). Provide complete, interconnected HTML files along with shared CSS and modular JavaScript files:
  1. `index.html` (The Atelier / Home: Immersive hero, brand essence, featured collections preview, interactive 3D showcase)
  2. `collections.html` (The Archive / Catalog: Filterable gallery of various handcrafted designs, collections, and lookbook grids)
  3. `story.html` (Namita's Journal / About: The intimate origin story of the homemade project, craftsmanship process, behind-the-scenes studio timeline)
  4. `contact.html` (Custom Orders & Connect: Bespoke jewelry inquiry form, care guide, and direct social integration)

THREE.JS & WEBGL INTEGRATION:
- Implement Three.js seamlessly into the DOM to elevate the tactile experience without overwhelming performance:
