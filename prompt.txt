Role: Expert Creative Frontend Developer & UI/UX Designer.

Objective: Build a unique, minimal, and deeply aesthetic multi-page HTML website for "Sunshine", an artisanal, handcrafted jewelry brand created by Namita as a personal homemade project.

Tech Stack & Constraints:
- Multi-page architecture using separate, linked HTML files (e.g., index.html, collections.html, story.html, contact.html). Do not build a single-page application.
- Use Tailwind CSS via CDN for layout and styling.
- Integrate Three.js for subtle, organic, and interactive 3D elements (e.g., floating abstract organic shapes, delicate metallic/glass-like particles, or subtle mouse-reactive background depth).
- Smooth, fluid page transitions and micro-animations (using vanilla JS or lightweight libraries like GSAP/Lenis via CDN) that feel natural, unhurried, and tactile.
- Imagery: Use high-resolution, curated Unsplash image URLs exclusively for all jewelry, lifestyle, and crafting photography. Do not generate images or use broken placeholders.
- Strict Exclusion: Do not include any specific color hex codes, RGB values, or named color palettes in the design; let the visual hierarchy rely on contrast, negative space, typography, and natural photographic tones.

Brand Identity & Content:
- Brand Name: Sunshine
- Creator: Namita
- Vibe: Intimate, organic, slow-made, minimal, gallery-like, and deeply personal. Avoid generic corporate e-commerce templates; treat each piece like a small wearable sculpture.
- Social Integration: Feature the official Instagram handle (@sunshine_handicraft_n) linking directly to https://www.instagram.com/sunshine_handicraft_n/ as the primary hub for inquiries, custom orders, and community connection.

Required Pages & Structure:
1. Home (index.html):
   - Atmospheric hero section featuring a subtle Three.js interactive canvas layered behind minimal, editorial typography.
   - Curated spotlight of signature handcrafted pieces with asymmetric, gallery-style grid layouts and smooth hover states.
   - Brief teaser of Namita's homemade philosophy and a seamless link to the Instagram showcase.

2. Collections (collections.html):
   - Filterable or categorized showcase of various handcrafted designs and collections (e.g., Everyday Minimal, Statement Pieces, Custom Creations).
   - Interactive lookbook feel with fluid image reveals, tactile hover animations, and direct "Inquire via Instagram" prompts for each piece instead of a generic checkout cart.

3. The Story (story.html):
   - An intimate, editorial narrative about Namita, the origin of Sunshine as a homemade passion project, and the slow, hands-on crafting process.
   - Asymmetric visual storytelling pairing Unsplash studio/crafting imagery with poetic, minimal typography.

4. Connect & Custom Orders (contact.html):
   - Minimalist layout guiding visitors to connect for bespoke designs and orders.
   - Prominent, interactive call-to-action directing users to https://www.instagram.com/sunshine_handicraft_n/, accompanied by a simple, clean inquiry form and FAQ regarding handmade timelines.

Design & UX Direction:
- Typography: Pair an expressive, organic editorial serif for headings with a clean, understated sans-serif for body text and navigation.
- Layout: Embrace generous whitespace, asymmetrical compositions, custom cursor interactions, and smooth scroll inertia.
- Ensure complete responsiveness across desktop and mobile viewports while maintaining the delicate, sculptural aesthetic.
