Build a creative, unique, and minimal multi-page HTML website for a handmade jewelry brand called "Sunshine" (Sunshine Jewelry), a small homemade handicraft project created by an artisan named Namita. 

### Core Requirements & Aesthetic Direction
- **Vibe & Feel:** The overall experience must feel deeply aesthetic, organic, natural, and personal—avoiding generic e-commerce templates. Keep the UI minimal with generous whitespace, refined editorial typography, and tactile, gentle micro-interactions.
- **No Color Prescriptions:** Do not enforce specific hex codes or color palettes; let the aesthetic feel warm, natural, and harmonious on its own.
- **Tech Stack:** Pure HTML5, Vanilla JavaScript, Tailwind CSS (via CDN), and Three.js (via CDN).
- **Imagery:** Strictly use real, high-resolution, aesthetic photography URLs from Unsplash (e.g., handmade jewelry, artisan crafting, natural textures, minimal portraits). Do NOT generate custom images, placeholders, or SVGs as image substitutes.

### Multi-Page Architecture
Create separate, interconnected HTML files with seamless page transitions and a persistent minimal navigation bar and footer:
1. `index.html` (Home): An editorial landing page introducing "Sunshine" by Namita, featuring an interactive Three.js hero canvas, a featured collection showcase, and a glimpse into the handmade process.
2. `collections.html` (Collections): A curated, filterable gallery showcasing Namita's handcrafted designs across various collections (e.g., Everyday Minimal, Nature-Inspired, Statement Pieces) with smooth hover animations and quick-view modals.
3. `story.html` (About / Craft): The personal story of Namita and how Sunshine started as a small homemade project, highlighting the slow-fashion philosophy, raw materials, and step-by-step handcrafting process.
4. `contact.html` (Inquiries & Custom Orders): A minimal custom-order inquiry form and direct connection hub for bespoke pieces.

### Three.js & Animation Guidelines
- **Three.js Integration:** Embed a subtle, lightweight, interactive Three.js WebGL scene (such as floating, gently rotating organic abstract forms, delicate metallic/beaded rings, or soft drifting light particles) that responds smoothly to cursor movement and scroll without overpowering the minimal layout.
- **Motion & Transitions:** Implement fluid scroll-triggered reveals, smooth parallax image framing, magnetic buttons, and soft page-fade transitions so every interaction feels natural and unhurried.

### Brand & Social Integration
- Prominently and tastefully feature Namita's story as the sole maker behind Sunshine.
- Include direct, working links and a curated social showcase section pointing to the official Instagram handle: https://www.instagram.com/sunshine_handicraft_n/
