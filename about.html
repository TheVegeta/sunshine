03 — The maker

The hands behind it.

Namita makes small things with a great deal of attention.

Chapter 01 — The beginning

It started at a kitchen table.

Sunshine wasn't a business plan. It was a way for Namita to keep making things with her hands after the rest of the day had gone quiet — a few wires, a few stones, an afternoon with nowhere else to be.

The first pieces were gifts. Then friends asked for one. Then friends of friends. Slowly, without ever being decided, the little project became an atelier.

Chapter 02 — The material

Choose slowly. Handle often.

Materials are picked for how they feel as much as how they look. A surface that catches light softly. A weight that sits right. A shape that seems to already know what it wants to become.

Nothing is bought in bulk just because it is available. If a material isn't right, it waits — or it never comes home at all.

Chapter 03 — The making

Every edge is a decision.

Each piece is formed, refined and finished by Namita herself. There is no mould and no assembly line. The small irregularities are not mistakes — they are the record of a real person choosing, adjusting, and trying again.

That is why no two pieces are exactly alike, and why the archive is always changing.
