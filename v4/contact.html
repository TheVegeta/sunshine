Before we meet

A few practical things, answered plainly.

Do I need to bring anything?
No. A photograph, a sketch or a piece you already own can help, but a conversation is enough to start.
Can I see pieces before visiting?
Yes. On a virtual consultation we can share photographs and short video of the pieces you are considering.
Can you resize a piece later?
Most rings can be resized later. Ask about the piece you have in mind and we will confirm what is possible.
How do I discuss a custom piece?
Mention it in your request. We will talk through metal, shape and budget before drawing anything.
