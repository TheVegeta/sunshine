Solstice ring in 18k gold

Price on request · Recycled gold

A soft-edged band carved in wax, cast in recycled gold and polished at the bench. The small differences between rings are part of the finish.

Metal finish

Selected: 18k Yellow Gold

Ring size

Cast to order. We confirm your size and finish by email.

Craftsmanship
Namita carves the band in wax, casts it in recycled gold, then files and polishes it. The inside is smooth; the outer edge stays softly uneven.
Materials & care
Recycled yellow gold, rose gold or gold vermeil. Remove before swimming or exercise, and keep the ring in its pouch when it is off.
Shipping
Each ring is cast to order. We confirm the dispatch window once size and finish are agreed. Insurance and a signature on delivery are included.

Worn every day

The profile sits low on the finger, so the ring works alone or alongside other pieces. It is meant to be worn, not kept in a box.
