The process

Four stages at the bench

  1. 01

    Carve the wax

    Namita cuts the shape in wax, leaving the tiny irregularities that make a hand-made edge.

  2. 02

    Choose the stone

    Stones are chosen for colour and clarity, then set aside for the right casting.

  3. 03

    Cast in gold

    The wax is replaced with recycled 18k gold. Each casting is checked for weight and surface before finishing.

  4. 04

    Polish by hand

    Files, papers and a final polish bring up the light. No two pieces leave the bench quite the same.

What we choose

Recycled gold

Every cast piece uses recycled gold rather than newly mined metal.

Traceable stones

We buy from suppliers who can name both the source and the cutting route.

Short runs

Short runs let Namita inspect each piece and avoid making more than we need.
