Our story

Made at home,
from the heart.

Sunshine is the homemade jewelry practice of Namita — a small, personal project where every piece is shaped slowly, thoughtfully, and by hand.

Handmade

One piece at a time

The maker

Hello, I'm Namita.

Sunshine began as a small home project — a way for me to create something meaningful with my hands. What started as curiosity and quiet experimentation slowly grew into a practice I fell in love with.

I make each piece myself, from my home workspace. There are no machines doing the work, no big production runs — just time, patience, and a deep appreciation for the little imperfections that make handmade things feel human.

For me, jewelry is more than an accessory. It's something personal — a small piece of warmth you can carry with you.

— Namita, maker of Sunshine

Slow fashion philosophy

Made slowly, meant to last.

01

Small batches

Every piece is made in limited quantities, often one at a time. This keeps the work personal and allows care in every detail.

02

Natural feeling

Materials, textures, and forms are chosen for their warmth and honesty — jewelry that feels grounded, organic, and easy to wear.

03

Human touch

The slight variations in handmade work are not flaws. They are proof that a real person shaped, finished, and cared for the piece.

The process

From raw idea to finished piece.

The making is quiet and deliberate. Each step gives the piece its character.

I.

Gather & imagine

Every piece starts with a feeling — a texture, a shape, or a mood. Namita selects materials that feel natural, warm, and right for the idea.

II.

Shape by hand

The form is slowly shaped and refined at the home workspace. This is where the piece finds its balance, weight, and personality.

III.

Refine & finish

Edges are softened, surfaces are polished, and details are checked by eye and hand. Nothing leaves the table until it feels complete.

IV.

Pack with care

Each order is wrapped thoughtfully, ready to become part of someone's everyday life — or a meaningful gift.
