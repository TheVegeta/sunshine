The spirit of Sunshine

Jewelry that feels personal, not produced.

Every Sunshine piece begins at Namita's home workspace — shaped slowly, finished thoughtfully, and made to be worn in real life. There is no factory, no rush, and no two pieces exactly alike.

The result is jewelry with warmth: small objects that carry the mark of human hands and the calm of intentional making.
